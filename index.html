Little Jam Cafe Privacy Policy

Draft · September 24, 2026

Little Jam Cafe is developed by Pockets Of Energy for players aged 13 and older. No game account is required.

Information we use

Progress, settings, and purchase receipts are stored on your device. Android may back up this data according to your settings. If you contact us, we use your email address and message to respond.

Google's advertising services may collect and share IP addresses, approximate location, device identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Ads are optional to watch, but ad requests may occur beforehand. Available privacy choices appear in game settings.

Google Play processes purchases. The game uses purchase records to verify and restore items; we do not receive your payment-card details. Google's processing and retention are described in Google's Privacy Policy. Providers may process data outside your country.

Storage and deletion

Clear the app's storage in Android to remove local data. In-game progress resets keep purchase records. Backups and Google's records are managed separately. Google's advertising SDK encrypts data in transit.

Proposed support policy: keep emails only as long as needed for support and legal obligations. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you provide. Depending on your location, additional privacy rights may apply.

Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns about information from a child under 13, email contact@pocketsofenergy.com.

We may update this policy when our practices change.
